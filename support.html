Support

Klaussy Desktop · Klaussy Loop · klaussy review · klaussy-agents

Getting help

Email stephanie@klaussy.com. Include your GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, or Azure DevOps account or organization name, and a link to the issue or pull request if your question is about a specific Klaussy Loop run or klaussy review.

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. It works for Klaussy Desktop, Klaussy Loop, klaussy review, and the klaussy-agents CLI, and you can see what others have reported.

klaussy is a small operation, so a reply can take a day or two.

For security issues, see the security page instead.

Quick answers (Klaussy Loop & klaussy review)

Change the agent or API key

Sign in at app.klaussy.com and update your settings. Only the account owner or an organization admin can change them.

Start a Klaussy Loop run

Connect your GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, or Azure DevOps repository at app.klaussy.com, hand Loop an issue, and approve the generated plan. Loop builds on a branch, runs tests and QA captures, and opens a pull or merge request.

QA takes screenshots and recordings of web, Electron and Android apps. Loop runs on iOS and native macOS apps too, but QA can’t take screenshots or screen recordings of them yet, so it runs their tests and keeps the terminal output as evidence instead. Screenshot and recording support for iOS and macOS is coming November 26, 2026.

Ask for a review

Comment /klaussy review on the pull request. You need write access to the repository.

Cancel a running review

Click the Cancel review button on the klaussy check, or comment /klaussy cancel on the pull request.

Review modes

Pick a mode in app.klaussy.com:

  • Automatic: klaussy reviews pull requests without being asked.
  • On request: klaussy only reviews when someone comments /klaussy review.
  • Every push: klaussy reviews again each time new commits are pushed.

Skip a pull request

Add the no-review label to the pull request.

"Credential rejected"

Your AI provider turned down the key you gave klaussy. It may be wrong, revoked or expired. Add a working key in app.klaussy.com.

"Out of credits"

Your AI provider account has run out of credit or quota. Add credit or raise the limit with your provider, then run the review or Loop task again.

A free review or Loop run didn't start

On Free, check whether you've used this month's allowance in app.klaussy.com. If you haven't, the overall limit everyone on Free shares has been reached: reviews start again the next day and Loop runs on the 1st of the month. A paid plan isn't affected by it. See Plans & Licensing.

Plans & Licensing

klaussy review: Free covers 20 public-repository and 10 private-repository reviews a month, up to 10 a day. Personal is $19 a month for a personal account, with up to 500 reviews a month and 100 a day. Pro is $29 a month, with up to 1,000 reviews a month and 200 a day. Team is $49 a month, with up to 2,000 reviews a month and 500 a day. Pro and Team take any number of members, and individuals can choose them too. Klaussy Loop: every Loop plan includes its klaussy review plan. Free Loop has 10 runs a month. Personal + Loop is $39 a month with 40 runs, Pro + Loop is $79 with 110, and Team + Loop is $149 with 240. Only Team + Loop keeps going past its runs, at $1 a run; the other plans start again the next month. Each account runs up to 3 Loop runs at once, and the rest wait their turn. Set it up at app.klaussy.com. If you subscribed before November 10, 2026, you keep your plan's price. Need higher limits or invoicing? Ask about Enterprise.

Free reviews and Free Loop runs each have an overall limit shared by everyone on Free: a daily one for reviews and a monthly one for Loop runs. When one is reached, free reviews or runs pause until the next day or month, even if your own allowance isn't used up. Paid plans aren't affected.

Klaussy Desktop & klaussy-agents: Klaussy Desktop is source-available under the Sustainable Use License (SUL 1.0) (free for personal use, development, and internal business use), and klaussy-agents is source-available under the Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-MIT).

Cancel your plan

Open the Stripe customer portal from app.klaussy.com and cancel there. If you're on the free trial, cancel before it ends and you won't be charged.

Uninstall or disconnect

For klaussy review on GitHub, go to your account or organization settings, open Installed GitHub Apps, choose klaussy review and uninstall it. For Klaussy Loop or other forges, disconnect your forge access in app.klaussy.com or your forge's settings. Uninstalling deletes your stored AI credential. If you're on a paid plan, cancel it as well.
