Support

klaussy review GitHub App

Getting help

Email stephanie@klaussy.com. Include your GitHub account or organization name, and a link to the pull request if your question is about a specific review.

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. It works for Klaussy Desktop, klaussy review and the CLI, and you can see what others have reported.

klaussy is a small operation, so a reply can take a day or two.

For security issues, see the security page instead.

Quick answers

Change the agent or API key

Sign in at app.klaussy.com and update the installation's settings. Only the account owner or an organization admin can change them.

Ask for a review

Comment /klaussy review on the pull request. You need write access to the repository.

Cancel a running review

Click the Cancel review button on the klaussy check, or comment /klaussy cancel on the pull request.

Review modes

Pick a mode in app.klaussy.com:

  • Automatic: klaussy reviews pull requests without being asked.
  • On request: klaussy only reviews when someone comments /klaussy review.
  • Every push: klaussy reviews again each time new commits are pushed.

Skip a pull request

Add the no-review label to the pull request.

"Credential rejected"

Your AI provider turned down the key you gave klaussy. It may be wrong, revoked or expired. Add a working key in app.klaussy.com.

"Out of credits"

Your AI provider account has run out of credit or quota. Add credit or raise the limit with your provider, then ask for the review again.

Plans

Free covers 20 public-repository and 5 private-repository reviews a month, up to 5 a day. Personal is $15 a month for a personal account, with up to 500 reviews a month and 100 a day. Team is $49 a month, with any number of members, up to 1,500 reviews a month and 300 a day; individuals can choose it too. Need higher limits or invoicing? Ask about Enterprise.

Cancel your plan

Open the Stripe customer portal from app.klaussy.com and cancel there. If you're on the free trial, cancel before it ends and you won't be charged.

Uninstall

In GitHub, go to your account or organization settings, open Installed GitHub Apps, choose klaussy review and uninstall it. Uninstalling deletes your stored AI credential. If you're on a paid plan, cancel it as well.
